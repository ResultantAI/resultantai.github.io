No, and anyone who guarantees this is selling something. What we can guarantee is that your content will meet the structural and signal criteria that LLMs use for retrieval. Citation outcomes depend on your category competitiveness, your existing authority, and execution of the distribution plan.

How is this different from traditional SEO? +

Traditional SEO optimizes for Google's ranking algorithm. AEO/GEO optimizes for how large language models retrieve and cite sources when generating answers. The technical surface is different: schema priorities, content structure, external citation surfaces, and ground-truth sources LLMs actually use.

What tools do you use? +

Ahrefs Brand Radar for citation tracking, manual retrieval testing across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity, Screaming Frog for technical audit, and proprietary scoring built on top of GSC data.

How fast will I see results? +

ChatGPT alone has hundreds of millions of weekly users running queries that used to go to Google.
