The problem

You're monitoring the news manually.
The news doesn't sleep.

Every morning, the same 15 tabs. Every afternoon, the fear you missed something. Every client call, the catch-up.

Signal Lag

You check sources once in the morning and hope nothing breaks in the afternoon. A regulatory announcement drops at 3pm. Your client sees it on LinkedIn at 4pm. You find out at 4:02pm when they call. The alert was your job.

Alert Fatigue

You signed up for Google Alerts. Now you get 200 emails a day about your clients' names, 190 of which are noise. You stopped reading them. The 10 that mattered went unread too. Noise is as dangerous as silence.

No Context with the Alert

Even when you catch the story, you still have to write the briefing. Summarize it. Add the relevant context. Explain what it means for the client. That takes 45 minutes per alert. You have six clients. Do the math.

How it works

Five steps from manual monitoring
to automated intelligence

01

Signal Taxonomy & Source Map

I document your monitored topics, entities, and relevance rules. Source list confirmed and tested for accessibility. Taxonomy built — the logic that determines what fires an alert vs. what gets filtered out.

02

Ingest Pipeline Build

Automated ingestion across all sources: RSS feeds, news APIs, web scrapers where needed. Deduplication logic prevents the same story from firing multiple times. Content normalized for AI processing.

03

AI Classification & Relevance Scoring

Each signal classified by topic, sentiment, urgency, and matched against your entity list. Claude generates a relevance score and, for matched signals, drafts a brief in your preferred format.

04

Alert Delivery & Distribution

Slack alerts configured with topic tags, urgency indicators, and AI draft text inline. Email digest formatted and scheduled. Client routing rules built if you serve multiple accounts.

05

Testing & Handoff

48-hour test run monitoring all sources. Alert volume calibrated — too many is as bad as too few. Documentation delivered: source list, taxonomy logic, how to add a source, and how to tune thresholds.
