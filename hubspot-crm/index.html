FAQ

Common questions

Will you delete my contact data? +

No. Every deduplication decision is reviewed before merging. I document every change and work from a clear audit trail. Nothing is deleted without explicit written approval from you.

My team has ignored HubSpot for 18 months. Is it too far gone? +

Probably not. The worst I have inherited was four years of unmanaged data from a company that imported every LinkedIn connection they ever had. We fixed it in 12 days. The key is agreeing on what data actually matters before we start cleaning anything.

Do you work directly in our HubSpot account? +

Yes. You add me as an admin user for the project duration. I do not need your password and you can remove access immediately after delivery.

Can you migrate us from Salesforce? +

Yes. The Advanced tier is designed for this. I handle field mapping, custom object migration, historical activity translation, and full validation before anything goes live. I know where the gaps are between Salesforce and HubSpot data models and I have done this migration specifically.

When does the delivery clock start? +

When you complete the intake requirements, not when you purchase. Most clients finish intake within 24 hours and are live well inside the delivery window.
