FAQ

Common questions

Will it sound like a robot? +

No. It uses Claude or GPT-4, not rule-based decision trees. It reads what the visitor types and responds conversationally. Most visitors do not realize they are talking to AI until the calendar invite lands in their inbox.

What happens when someone asks something the bot doesn't know? +

The bot handles off-topic questions gracefully and routes them to a human handoff or email capture. You define the fallback behavior at kickoff. Nothing gets dropped.

Do I need a developer to install it? +

No. Delivered as a single embed snippet — one line of code in your site footer. Works on Webflow, WordPress, Squarespace, or custom HTML. If you want me to handle the install directly, I can.

Can it book meetings directly on my calendar? +

Yes. Standard and Advanced tiers include calendar integration. Visitors pick a time without leaving the chat. Both sides receive a confirmation. Supports Calendly, HubSpot Meetings, Cal.com, and Google Calendar.

Do I own the chatbot when the project is done? +

Yes, 100%. Your OpenAI or Anthropic account, your CRM connection, your embed code. Nothing is hosted on my infrastructure or tied to a monthly fee from me.
