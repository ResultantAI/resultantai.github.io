What gets built

Two things, mostly

The trade matters less than the workflow. These two cover most of what field businesses actually need.

Voice AI call handling
Inbound calls answered around the clock, urgent work separated from routine, caller details captured into your CRM or field service software, and appointments booked. Every call opens with a disclosure that it is an automated call, and reaching a person is always available.
Operations automation
Digital ticketing to replace paper, dispatch and scheduling workflows, invoice and billing generation, accounting sync, and the recurring reports nobody has time to assemble. Built on Make.com and n8n so you can see and change what it does.
Trades covered
HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, landscaping, tree services, pool services, construction, propane, and materials or logistics operations.
Systems it plugs into
ServiceTitan, Jobber, HubSpot, Airtable, QuickBooks and most things with an API. If it does not have one, that is worth knowing before you buy rather than after.
