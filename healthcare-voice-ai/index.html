The problem

Patients can't book. Staff can't keep up.

Your front desk handles calls, check-ins, paperwork, and rescheduling simultaneously. The phone doesn't stop. The EHR doesn't update itself.

After-Hours Calls Go to Voicemail

Your front desk leaves at 5pm. Patient calls at 7pm to book a Monday appointment. They leave a voicemail. You call back Tuesday. They already booked with someone else. After-hours is your highest-volume booking window.

EHR Never Updated in Real-Time

Staff take down appointment details on paper or sticky notes during the call. EHR gets updated later — sometimes hours later, sometimes incorrectly. Double-bookings and missed appointments follow. Your schedule is never actually accurate.

Language Is a Barrier

A quarter of your patient calls are in Spanish. Your English-only front desk transfers them, puts them on hold, or apologizes. Those patients book less, cancel more, and don't refer. The gap is preventable.
