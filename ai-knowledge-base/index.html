The problem

Ask your CS team, your ops lead, and your newest hire the same question. You will get three different answers. The SOP was updated in January. Not everyone got the memo. The old version is still in the wiki.

Your best answers live in your top employees' heads. When they are on vacation, on a call, or gone entirely — that knowledge disappears. Every new hire re-learns the same things from scratch.

How it works

From buried docs to instant answers
in five steps

01

Document Audit & Chunking Strategy

I review your document library, define chunking logic for each source type, and set up the vector store. Sources with formatting issues or outdated content are flagged before ingestion begins.

02

Ingestion Pipeline Build

All documents imported, chunked, and embedded. Drive or Notion sync configured — new files flow in automatically without manual re-upload. Ingestion pipeline tested with a sample of your content.

03

Claude Integration & Prompt Engineering

RAG pipeline built with Claude. Prompts engineered for your content: citation formatting, confidence thresholds, graceful fallback when an answer is not in the knowledge base, and tone calibration.

04

Bot Deployment & Auth

Slack bot installed in your workspace. Web widget deployed if applicable. Role-based filtering configured so users only see content they are permitted to access. Live immediately after deployment.

05

Testing & Handoff

50-question test suite run against real team queries — including edge cases and questions the bot should decline to answer. Analytics configured. Documentation delivered for ingestion pipeline and prompt logic.
