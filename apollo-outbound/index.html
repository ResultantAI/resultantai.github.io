The Problem

Your outbound looks like everyone else's outbound.

Template Fatigue

Buyers have seen every "quick question" and "just checking in" variation. They delete without reading.

The average B2B buyer receives 120 cold emails per week. Generic openers, copy-paste value props, and fake personalization ("I noticed you're a VP at {{company}}") get archived in under 2 seconds. AI personalization that references real signals is the only thing that breaks through.

No Deliverability Foundation

Your emails land in spam before a buyer ever has the chance to delete them.

SPF, DKIM, and DMARC misconfiguration. Sending from your primary domain. No warmup. No inbox rotation. These are the reasons your outbound "doesn't work" — and none of them have anything to do with your copy.

Disconnected Stack

Replies come in, reps don't know, deals don't get created. The whole loop breaks.

Apollo fires an email. A buyer responds at 7am. The rep doesn't check Apollo until noon. The window is gone. HubSpot has no record of the conversation. The sequence keeps running. You don't know what's actually working.

FAQ

Common questions

Is this just Zapier connecting Apollo to HubSpot? +

No. The AI personalization layer is what separates this from a template sequence. Claude researches each contact — recent LinkedIn post, company announcement, hiring signal, tech stack — and writes a unique opening line. That is the difference between a 0.8% and a 4.2% reply rate.

Do I need an Apollo account already? +

No. I will set up your workspace, configure the team plan, and walk you through billing before we start. If you already have Apollo, I audit the current setup and rebuild from there. Either way, you own the account.

What if my domain reputation is low? +

The Email Deliverability Setup add-on covers this. SPF, DKIM, and DMARC configured. A separate sending subdomain warmed up over 2 to 3 weeks before the full sequence launches. This is not optional if you want to land in the inbox — it is the foundation.

How many emails per day will this send? +

We start conservatively — typically 50 to 100 per day — and ramp based on deliverability signals. By week three, most accounts are running 150 to 300 per day per inbox without deliverability issues. I document the ramp schedule at kickoff so you know exactly what to expect each week.

What happens when someone replies? +

Every reply is logged to HubSpot automatically. With the AI Reply Handling add-on, Claude classifies the reply — interested, not now, wrong person, unsubscribe — and routes accordingly. Interested replies create a HubSpot task and Slack alert so your rep can follow up within the hour.
