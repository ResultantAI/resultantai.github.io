Adoption happened. Training didn't.

Your company rolled out Copilot or ChatGPT. People ask it questions. Very few hand it work. The difference is the same skill that makes someone a good manager: clear context, a clear audience, a clear standard for done. Managing AI is surprisingly similar to managing people. We teach it that way.

What your team keeps

A shared language for what AI can and cannot do today, from chat up to agents.
A method for scoring any process on five dimensions, with three verdicts: Delegate, Redesign First, Keep Human.
A ranked list of candidate processes, scored by the people who own the work. It is the answer ready for the day someone asks "show me your roadmap, where can we automate."

Three formats

Where Agents Fit

2 hours. On site or hybrid.

Prompting as leadership, the levels of AI capability that lead up to agents, and a live agent demo. Then the room scores its own processes and sorts each one: Delegate, Redesign First, or Keep Human.

Live Build

1 hour. Remote.

We take one process from your team's list and build and publish the agent live, start to finish, so everyone sees the whole path from idea to working tool.

Build Lab

At least 2 hours. Hands on.

Each participant builds and publishes an agent for their own process, with us in the room to get them unstuck.
