The Problem

Users who don't reach their first win in 7 days rarely come back.

Day-0 Churn

40% of trial users never log in a second time. They signed up curious. They left confused.

The gap between "I signed up" and "I got value" is where most SaaS churn happens. A welcome email sent 3 minutes after signup is not onboarding. It is a placeholder. Behavioral triggers, milestone tracking, and timely interventions are what close the gap.

CS Blind Spots

Your CS team doesn't know an account is in trouble until it's already too late to save it.

No login in 4 days. Setup wizard at 20%. Core feature never touched. These signals are visible in your product data. Without automated alerting, they stay invisible until the cancellation email arrives. By then, 10 conversations ago is when you needed to intervene.

Generic Onboarding

Every user gets the same sequence regardless of their role, plan, or stated use case.

A solo founder using your product for personal projects has completely different needs than an enterprise VP onboarding a 50-person team. The same Day 3 email sent to both converts neither. Segmented paths based on signup data and behavior convert both.

FAQ

Common questions

What email platforms do you work with? +

HubSpot, Intercom, Customer.io, ActiveCampaign, and Klaviyo. If you're using a different platform, tell me at kickoff and I'll confirm compatibility. The sequence logic is the same regardless of platform.

What if we don't know our activation rate? +

Most clients don't. I start by instrumenting the activation funnel so you have a baseline within the first two weeks. We build the onboarding system and measurement together — you'll have real numbers before the first optimization review.

Can this work if our product requires a human setup call? +

Yes. The automation handles everything before and after the call: pre-call setup checklist, post-call follow-up sequence, and milestone tracking once they start configuring the product. The call stays in the journey — the automation takes away everything around it.

How do you define "activated"? +

You define it. I document it. Common definitions: first core feature used, first data imported, first output generated, first team member invited. Whatever predicts long-term retention in your product is what we optimize for — and we validate it against your existing cohort data if available.

What about enterprise accounts that need white-glove onboarding? +

The Segmented Onboarding Paths add-on covers this. Enterprise accounts get a different sequence — more touchpoints, CS intro call trigger, executive check-in at day 14. Self-serve accounts get product-led guidance. Both paths built and tested separately.
