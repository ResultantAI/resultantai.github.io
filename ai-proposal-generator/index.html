How it works

Five steps from blank doc
to automated proposal pipeline

01

Proposal Audit & Template Design

I review your existing proposals and extract your structure: the sections you always include, how you describe deliverables, how you present pricing, and the language that closes deals. Template skeleton built and reviewed before any prompts are written.

02

Intake Form Build

Intake form designed around the minimum information needed to write an accurate proposal. Too few questions and the output is generic. Too many and your sales team skips it. Built in Typeform, Tally, or Google Form -- your choice.

03

Prompt Engineering & Voice Calibration

Claude prompts engineered per template with strict output structure. Voice calibration run against your sample proposals -- the AI generates in your tone, uses your section headings, and avoids language you never use. Test proposals reviewed together until you approve the output quality.

04

CRM Integration & Output Pipeline

Proposal generated, Google Doc created, stored in Drive, CRM deal created with all fields populated, email draft queued or sent automatically. Each step confirmed end-to-end before handoff.

05

Testing & Handoff

5 test proposals generated across different service types and client profiles. Edge cases tested. Handoff package: how to add a new template, update pricing logic, and modify the voice calibration prompt without rebuilding anything.
