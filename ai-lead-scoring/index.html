FAQ

Common questions

What CRMs do you work with? +

HubSpot is the primary platform. I also build for Salesforce and Pipedrive. The score and reasoning are written back as standard contact properties so they appear in your existing list views, sequences, and reports — nothing new to learn.

Do I need historical deal data? +

It helps significantly but is not required. With closed/won data, I can calibrate the model against what actually converted. Without it, I build from your ICP definition and refine from there. Most models improve meaningfully within 30 days of live scoring data.

Will my reps actually use it? +

The score appears on the contact record and optionally triggers a Slack alert with the reasoning summary. No new tool. No new login. The score lives where your reps already work — and they can sort their pipeline by score in one click.

How is this different from HubSpot's built-in lead scoring? +

HubSpot's predictive scoring is a black box trained on generic data. This model is built specifically on your ICP, your historical deals, and your behavioral signals. The reasoning is written in plain language so your team understands and trusts it — which is the only thing that makes a scoring system actually change rep behavior.

What happens as my ICP changes? +

The Monthly Scoring Calibration add-on covers this. Each month the model is reviewed against your actual close rate by score tier, weights are adjusted, and a performance report is delivered. Without the add-on, I document the scoring criteria clearly enough that your team can request adjustments as part of a future engagement.
